## 1.2.0 — Cathedrals, sacred art, chant and Mass gameplay

- Sculpted Our Lady and St. Joseph models with robe folds, faces, halos, joined hands, Child Jesus and lily details.
- Three framed public-domain paintings with artist credits and craftable recipes.
- Three complete public-domain chant recordings as streaming jukebox discs, with subtitles and source/rights/hash manifest.
- Guided persistent Mass sessions in the traditional and modern orders, preparation and vestment checks, nearby participation, ownership and atomic offertory gifts.
- Craftable wine cruets with returned bottles.
- Peaceful persistent monk NPCs with a custom habit/cowl model, home wandering, danger avoidance and six supply offers.
- Naturally generated furnished Gothic cathedrals in temperate biomes, three resident monks and stocked sacristies; cathedral/monk commands and vanilla structure location.
- Stocked the chapel for Mass and chant, added paintings and jukebox, and expanded the in-game Field Guide to 22 pages.
- Expanded native Minecraft tests to 26, resource checks to cover media provenance/audio/worldgen, and native client validation to 132 models and 62 pages.
